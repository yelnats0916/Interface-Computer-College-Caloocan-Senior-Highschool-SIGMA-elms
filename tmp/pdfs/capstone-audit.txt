SYSTEM 
 
This capstone project, "SIGMA: An AI-Integrated E-Learning Management System," aims to 
modernize the educational experience for the Senior High School department of Interface 
Computer College Caloocan. The system addresses common challenges in traditional schooling, 
such as manual grade tracking, scattered student information, and the need for more personalized 
learning tools. It provides a secure, all-in-one platform for students, teachers, and School 
Administrator. 
 
 
The system features Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data analytics to give clear insights into 
student performance. By monitoring grades and attendance on time, the platform helps teachers 
identify students who may need extra help and allows School Administrator to track overall 
academic trends. This data-driven approach helps the school make better decisions and improve 
the way lessons are delivered.

submit assignments, and track their own grades instantly. This eliminates confusion and helps

technology, this project supports the school’s goal of providing high-quality, innovative 
education for its Senior High School students.

CHAPTER I 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
Technology is now a big part of education. Schools use computers and the internet to help 
students learn better and to make daily work easier. However, many schools still struggle with 
managing student records, computing grades, and handling online classes all at the same time. 
When teachers do these tasks manually or use different program s for each task, it takes a lot of 
time and often leads to mistakes. In the Philippines, different levels of education have adopted 
different digital tools to support learning. College institutions have long been using dedicated 
Learning Management Systems such as Moodle and Canvas, which offer complete tools for course 
management, grading, and student monitoring. Grade school levels, on the other hand, largely 
depend on simpler and more accessible platforms like Google Classroom, which are easy to use 
but limited in academic management features. Senior High School, however, tells a different story. 
Even among private SHS institutions, most rely on the same general -purpose tools, primarily 
Google Classroom or basic versions of Moodle, that were never designe d with the specific 
structure and demands of Philippine Senior High School in mind. As a result, SHS schools are left 
using platforms borrowed from other educational levels, without a dedicated system that truly fits 
their needs. Because of these limitations, teachers in Senior High School still face many difficulties 
in managing academic tasks. Computing grades for SHS is particularly complicated because each 
subject requires evaluating students across multiple components: Written Works, Performance 
Tasks, and quarterly assessments following the grading formula prescribed by the Department of 
Education. When teachers compute these manually or transfer data between different software, the 
process becomes slow and prone to human error. Students also suffer because they do not receive 
immediate feedback on their performance, and School Administrator finds it difficult to monitor 
overall student progress in real time. Without a dedicated system built around the structure of 
Philippine SHS, these problems continue to affect the quality of teaching and learning. 
Interface Computer College Caloocan is a private Senior High School institution offering three 
strands under the DepEd K to 12 curriculum: Accountancy, Business and Management (ABM), 
Home Economics (HE), General Academic Strand (GAS), Humanities and Social  Sciences 
(HUMSS), and Information and Communications Technology (ICT). As a private school, ICC

Caloocan has the flexibility to adopt its own digital learning solutions beyond what public schools 
are provided by DepEd. However, like many private SHS institutions, the school currently utilizes 
a Moodle -based e -learning platform called FLInt, while functional as a general learning 
management system, was not purpose-built for the demands of Senior High School education. The 
existing system allows teachers to upload learning materials and administer basic assessments, but 
it lacks automated grade computation, real -time student performance monitoring, and AI -
integrated tools. As a result, teachers still compute grades manually, maintain records across 
separate applications, and make academic decisions without the support of intelligent monitoring 
tools problems that directly affect the efficiency of teaching and the academic experience of 
students. 
To address these challenges, this study proposes SIGMA: an AI -Integrated E -Learning 
Management System with Student Information and Grade Monitoring Analytics, developed 
specifically for the Senior High School department of Interface Computer College Calooc an. 
SIGMA is designed to fill the technological gap in Philippine SHS by providing a unified platform 
that combines the accessibility of familiar learning tools with the complete academic management 
capabilities of a dedicated system. It integrates an AI -powered assistant known as Sigma Bot, 
automated grade computation following the DepEd grading formula, real -time student 
performance monitoring, and AI -assisted content generation, all purpose -built for the structure, 
curriculum, and needs of Senior High Sc hool. Rather than relying on separate and disconnected 
applications, SIGMA brings everything together in one secure, intelligent, and easy -to-use 
platform for students, teachers, School Administrator, and school management.

experienced by the Senior High School department. Although the school already utilizes a general-
purpose Learning Management System for uploading topics and giving quizzes, it lacks the

During daily operations, teachers must encode scores manually in  Excel and compute grades one 
by one. This process often takes up to one hour per class section, which significantly increases the 
risk of human error and delays the release of results. The school also does not have a fully 
integrated system that combines attendance, records, grading, and learning management in one 
platform, so some teachers use multiple applications to complete their tasks. 
Another concern is the lack of smart tools for student monitoring and feedback. Teachers must 
update records and grading sheets manually whenever there are new subjects or performance tasks, 
while students and parents often need to ask how grades are computed and what areas need 
improvement. Because of this, the current system does not provide automatic feedback, 
performance analytics, or real-time progress reports to support faster academic decisions. 
 
Purpose and Description 
 
Interface Computer College Caloocan currently utilizes FLInt, a Moodle-based e-learning 
platform that provides basic functions such as uploading learning materials and administering 
assessments. While the platform exists, it lacks the academic management capabilities that Senior 
High School education demands particularly automated grade computation, real -time student 
performance monitoring, and intelligent analytics tools. Teachers continue to rely on manual 
processes and separate applications, resulting in inefficiencies that directly affect both instruction 
and student outcomes. 
SIGMA is developed to address these gaps by providing a unified, AI-integrated platform purpose-
built for the Senior High School department of Interface Computer College Caloocan. Unlike 
general-purpose learning management systems, SIGMA is designed around the specific structure 
of the DepEd K to 12 curriculum, supporting strand and track configuration, subject management, 
section assignment, and automated grade computation based on the weight distribution prescribed 
by DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015. The system supports four user roles Student, Teacher, and School 
Administrator, each with clearly defined access and capabilities. School Administrator configures 
the school's academic structure, manage teacher and student accounts, and upload official school-
wide learning materials. Teachers manage their assigned classrooms, upload learning materials, 
create and assign quizzes, assignments, activities, and performance tasks, take attendance, and 

=== PDF PAGE 11 ===
11  
monitor student performance in real time through an automated gradebook that computes Written 
Works, Performance Tasks, and Quarterly Assessment scores following the prescribed DepEd 
grading formula. Students access all available learning materials, submit their outputs, view their 
grades broken down by component, and track their academic p rogress anytime. The School 
Administrator oversees the entire system, manages accounts, monitors system and storage usage, 
and configures the AI integration settings. Artificial Intelligence is integrated throughout SIGMA 
in multiple ways. Sigma Bot, an AI-powered assistant accessible to students and teachers, provides 
intelligent chat support scoped to ELMS and ICC-related topics, helping users navigate the system 
and understand their learning materials. When a teacher uploads a PDF document, Sigma Bot 
automatically extracts its content and uses it as context, allowing students to ask specific questions 
about uploaded materials directly. For content creation, teachers can use AI-assisted generation to 
draft quizzes, assignments, and activities complete with scoring rubrics, which must be reviewed 
and approved before publishing. The Grades section of SIGMA provides both teachers and 
students with a comprehensive view of academic performance teachers have access to an analytics 
dashboard that displays class-wide performance trends, flags students showing signs of academic 
difficulty, and provides AI-generated prescriptive recommendations alongside a full gradebook for 
encoding and monitoring individual student scores, while students can view their own grade 
breakdown per component, track their performance trends across quarters, and receive 
personalized feedback from Sigma Bot based on their academic stan ding. The AI integration is 
managed and configured by the School Administrator, who controls system prompt settings, API 
access, and usage monitoring to ensure the AI remains focused on educational and institutional 
purposes. By bringing together e -learning, student information management, automated grade 
computation, attendance monitoring, and AI -driven analytics into one secure and easy -to-use 
platform, SIGMA aims to reduce the administrative workload of teachers, improve the academic 
experience of students, and help school leaders make faster and more informed decisions all within 
a system purpose-built for the unique structure and academic demands of the Senior High School 
curriculum.

Statement of the Problem 
 
 
 
 
General Problem 
 
 
The Senior High School department of Interface Computer College Caloocan, currently relies 
on a general -purpose, Moodle -based platform (FLInt) and manual processes that lack specific 
academic management tools required by the Philippine Senior High School Curriculum . This 
disconnection between the existing technology and the specific needs of the SHS department leads 
to administrative inefficiencies, delayed feedback for students, and difficulty in monitoring overall 
academic progress. 
 
 
 
Specific Problems 
 
 
The researchers identified the following critical issues within the current setup: 
 
 
• Manual Administrative Workload: Teachers are burdened by the need to manually 
encode scores in Excel and compute grades individually, a time-consuming process that 
increases the high risk of human error. 
• Delayed Academic Feedback: Because the current system lacks real-time monitoring and 
analytics, students do not receive immediate feedback on their performance, and parents are 
often left unclear on how grades are computed. 
• Lack of Integrated Support: Both students and teachers lack access to intelligent, real -

applications for their daily academic tasks. 
• Scattered Records and Information: The school lacks a unified platform to consolidate

to track long-term academic trends effectively.

The main goal of this project is to develop SIGMA, an AI-Integrated E-Learning Management

• Login 
 
Users authenticate by entering their email address or Login ID together with their 
password. 
 
• Credential Validation 
 
The SIGMA ELMS validates the entered email address or Login ID and password 
to determine whether they match the stored database records. 
 
• Login ID Verification 
 
The SIGMA ELMS determines whether the entered email address or Login 
ID exists in the database. If no corresponding record is found, the SIGMA 
ELMS records the failed attempt and displays an invalid login message.

• Password Verification 
 
If the entered Login ID is found in the database, the SIGMA ELMS verifies 
the password associated with the account. If the password is incorrect, the 
SIGMA ELMS records the failed attempt and displays a message indicating 
that the login credentials are invalid. 
 
 
• Google reCAPTCHA Verification 
 
Google reCAPTCHA is required after repeated failed login attempts to help 
prevent automated access attempts. Successful verification allows the user to 
continue with the login process. 
 
 
• reCAPTCHA Triggering 
When the number of failed attempts exceeds the defined threshold, Google 
reCAPTCHA is displayed on the next login attempt. 
 
• Verification Result 
 
If reCAPTCHA verification is unsuccessful, the SIGMA ELMS prevents 
access and displays a corresponding verification error message. The user is 
then required to repeat the verification process before continuing.

If the entered password remains incorrect after eight consecutive failed attempts,

Technical Background 
CHAPTER II 
METHODOLOGY 
 
 
This chapter gives an overview of the technology used to build SIGMA: An AI-Integrated E-
Learning Management System. The system is made to help the Senior High School department 
of Interface Computer College Caloocan by putting school records, online classes, and smart 
monitoring tools in one place. 
The SIGMA system is a web-based platform that works on computers, tablets, and smartphones. 
 
It has different features for Students, Teachers, and School Administrator. The front part of the 
system is where students view their lessons and grades, while teachers use it to manage their 
classes. The back part of the system uses an API for Artificial Intelligence (Sigma Bot) to 
automatically compute grades based on DepEd rules and provide reports on student performance. 
This setup keeps all student information safe in one database and uses smart tools to make school 
work easier and faster for everyone. 
 
 
 
Technologies to be used 
 
 
The proponents primarily use desktop computers and laptops for planning, organizing, and 
 
writing the documentation. These devices are also the main tools for coding the SIGMA system, 
managing the database, and setting up the web-based platform. 
Smartphones are used to test the responsive design of the system. This ensures that the layout

extensions to write, test, and debug the system’s code efficiently.

clean, and responsive layout that looks good on both desktop and mobile screens without writing

Requirements Documentation 
 
The requirements documentation of SIGMA: An AI-Integrated E-Learning Management 
System with Student Information and Grade Monitoring Analytics defines the functional and 
non-functional requirements necessary to support the academic operations of the Senior High 
School department of Interface Computer College Caloocan. Based on the interactions presented 
in the system’s context diagram, the platform is designed to address existing challenges such as 
manual grade computation, scattered student records, delayed academic feedback, and the lack of 
an integrated academic system. It identifies the needs of the primary users: students, teachers, 
and the school administrator and specifies essential system functionalities, including secure user 
authentication with role-based access, a centralized Student Information System for managing 
student records, and a web-based e-learning platform for delivering instructional materials and 
managing class activities. SIGMA also includes an assessment module for quizzes, assignments, 
and performance tasks, as well as an automated grade monitoring feature that computes grades 
based on the Department of Education grading formula. In addition, SIGMA integrates an AI-
powered assistant (Sigma Bot) that provides learning support, generates academic content, and 
analyzes student performance using available system data. 
Furthermore, SIGMA incorporates non-functional requirements to ensure efficiency, security, 
and usability within a real-world academic environment. SIGMA is designed to deliver reliable 
performance through real-time data processing, allowing multiple users to access the platform 
simultaneously with minimal delay. Security measures such as credential validation, account 
lockout mechanisms, and CAPTCHA verification are implemented to protect sensitive academic 
information and ensure controlled access to the system. The platform also emphasizes usability 
through a responsive and user-friendly interface that is accessible across various devices, 
including desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones, with compatibility across major web 
browsers. In terms of reliability and maintainability, SIGMA is structured to minimize errors, 
maintain data consistency, and support future enhancements, particularly in AI integration and 
analytics capabilities. These requirements serve as the foundation for the system’s design and 
iterative development under the Agile methodology, ensuring that SIGMA effectively improves 
academic management, enhances the learning experience, and supports data-driven decision-
making within the institution.
