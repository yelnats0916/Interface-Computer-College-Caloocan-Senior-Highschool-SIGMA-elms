# DepEd-based SHS catalog

Planning catalog; not an approved ICC class program. ICT/HE specialization coverage and GAS elective choices require ICC confirmation.

## Scope and sources

- Capstone documentation PDF page 7: ABM, GAS, HUMSS, ICT and HE. No STEM, Arts and Design, Sports, or K–10 subjects are included.
- Curriculum framework: original Core / Applied / Specialized structure used in the document, not the separate Strengthened SHS program.
- [ICC SHS programs](https://home1.interface.edu.ph/index.php/programs/senior-high-school): programs are listed, but a Caloocan subject/semester checklist and lesson outlines were not found.
- [DepEd Academic Track catalog](https://www.deped.gov.ph/k-to-12/about/k-to-12-basic-education-curriculum/academic-track/)
- [DepEd grading policy, Table 5, PDF page 13](https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/DO_s2015_08.pdf)

## Planning decisions

- All records are drafts. Semester placements are proposed and must be checked against ICC’s approved class program.
- The guides set competencies and time allocations; they do not mandate one universal number of LMS topics or materials. Topic units below group related competencies. One information-only learning-guide record is attached to each topic.
- Units use the user-approved provisional application convention (20 hours = 1 unit, 80 hours = 3 units, 160 hours = 6 units), pending ICC confirmation. Learning hours are stored separately: typically 80 hours, with 20 hours per PE semester; candidate TVL qualifications use their guide hours.
- Applied subjects have separate strand records so gradebook weights and track-specific Filipino variants remain unambiguous.
- GAS Humanities/Social Science/elective choices are candidates, not a claim that ICC selected them.
- Candidate CSS and Food and Beverage Services are based on DepEd guides and ICC’s TESDA program page; TESDA listings do not establish the actual SHS offering. Other ICT/HE qualifications are not invented.
- No subject is assigned to a section; none exist. The catalog can be assigned after its school-specific details are confirmed and it is published.
- No attachments, video URLs, quizzes, scores, due dates, or submissions are created.
