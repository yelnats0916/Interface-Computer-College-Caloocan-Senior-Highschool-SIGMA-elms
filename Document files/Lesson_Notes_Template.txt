--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY DEFINITIONS & PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Physical Layer: Transmits raw bit streams across physical cables and
  wireless frequencies.
- Data Link Layer: Handles MAC physical addressing, frame encapsulation,
  and switch operations.
- Network Layer: Responsible for IP logical addressing, packet routing,
  and router decisions.
- Transport Layer: Manages segmentation, port multiplexing, and reliable
  TCP/UDP connections.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE RULES & FACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Routers operate at Layer 3; standard network switches operate at Layer 2.
- TCP ensures reliable ordered delivery; UDP provides connectionless fast
  streaming.
