Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding student accounts, credentials, and portals.

What is my ID and Email?

Your official ID and Email are provided upon enrollment. If you need to retrieve these credentials, please visit the Registrar's Office with a valid ID.

What is the default password?

Default passwords are typically your Surname followed by your registration id given in elms by the admin. If you are still unsure of your password details, please contact the Registrar's Office or your designated ELMS administrator.

How do I reset a forgotten password?

For security reasons, password resets are handled in person. Please visit the Admin Office to request a manual reset of your account credentials.

Why is my account inactive?

Accounts may be set to inactive if there are pending enrollment issues, unconfirmed subject loads, or if the account has not been enrolled for consecutive semesters. To reactivate your account, please coordinate with the Registrar's Office to confirm your enrollment status.

Why is my account locked, and how do I unlock it?

Accounts are automatically locked after multiple consecutive failed password attempts to safeguard your account against unauthorized access. If your login displays "The account is locked," please visit the Admin Office or ICT Department with your valid school ID to verify your identity and have an administrator unlock your account.

What is Google Authenticator (2FA) and how do I use it?

Google Authenticator provides an extra layer of two-factor authentication (2FA) security to protect your student or faculty account. On your first login, you will be prompted to scan a QR barcode with the Google Authenticator app on your smartphone. On all subsequent logins, simply open the app and enter the 6-digit verification code.

What should I do if I lost my phone, deleted the app, or my 2FA code is not working?

If you deleted the app or got a new phone while online, you can click "Lost code or deleted app? Reset 2FA" directly on the 2FA verification screen to immediately generate a fresh QR barcode. If you are offline or need assistance, please visit the Admin Office or ICT Department with your valid school ID to have an administrator reset your 2FA.

Contact Support

Reach out if you still cannot sign in, your account details do not match, your password reset does not work, or you need help confirming enrollment-related access.

You may visit the ICT Department during school hours, or open the official contact page for registrar and school support concerns.

Terms of Use & Privacy Agreement

Mandatory institutional onboarding agreement for SIGMA ELMS

Compliance

Welcome to Interface Computer College ELMS

Please read and review all 10 institutional policy sections below. You must scroll to the bottom of this document before you can unlock and check the agreement box.

1

Academic Integrity & Authentic Authorship

All submitted assignments, laboratory exercises, research papers, quizzes, and examination answers must strictly represent your own original, authentic intellectual work. Plagiarism in any form, unauthorized collaboration during individual assessments, purchasing or copying academic deliverables, and submitting fraudulent records are grave offenses subject to disciplinary action under the ICC Senior High School Student Handbook.

2

Responsible AI & Generative Technology Guidelines

Integrated artificial intelligence tools and tutoring assistants are provided exclusively for conceptual comprehension, exploratory learning, and study guidance. Submitting AI-generated text, solutions, or code without faculty authorization and explicit attribution is prohibited. Course faculty retain full authority to require manual defense, proctored verification, or authentic revisions of any submitted assessment.

3

Account Security & Google Authenticator (2FA)

Your portal account, institutional Login ID, password, and Google Authenticator Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) security keys are strictly personal and non-transferable. You are accountable for all activities conducted under your credentials. Sharing account credentials, logging into another user's workstation, or attempting to bypass security authentication constitutes an institutional violation.

4

Data Privacy Act Compliance (Republic Act No. 10173)

Interface Computer College collects, processes, and protects your educational records, attendance logs, assessment submissions, and gradebook data in strict adherence to the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Information stored within SIGMA ELMS is processed solely for legitimate academic administration, curriculum tracking, and Department of Education (DepEd) compliance, protected by organizational and technical safeguards.

5

Acceptable IT Infrastructure & Network Utilization

Users must not perform automated vulnerability scanning, reverse engineering, unauthorized database querying, network denial of service, script injection, or system tampering against the SIGMA ELMS servers. All portal interactions are logged in institutional audit trails for accountability and forensic integrity.

6

Intellectual Property & Courseware Distribution Rights

All learning materials, presentations, curriculum modules, assessment questionnaires, and digital assets published on SIGMA ELMS remain the proprietary intellectual property of Interface Computer College and respective instructors. Unauthorized reproduction, public distribution, or commercial reposting of institutional materials outside the portal is strictly forbidden.

7

Digital Code of Conduct & Online Classroom Etiquette

All communications across SIGMA ELMS discussion boards, submissions, notifications, and portal feedback channels must uphold the highest standards of mutual respect and civility. Harassment, abusive language, discrimination, disruption of online sessions, or posting defamatory material will result in immediate portal sanctions and referral to the Office of Student Affairs.

8

Continuous System Audit Trails & Activity Telemetry

The SIGMA ELMS platform automatically records system activity logs, including login timestamps, IP addresses, device identifiers, session lifecycles, assessment submission events, and security access attempts. These audit logs serve as objective verification data for academic verification, grading dispute resolution, and regulatory accreditation.

9

Device Security & Public Workstation Logout

When accessing SIGMA ELMS from computer laboratories, shared family devices, or public computer terminals, users are strictly required to explicitly log out after finishing their session. Leaving active portal sessions unattended on shared workstations compromises student privacy and creates vulnerability to unauthorized profile manipulation.

10

Disciplinary Sanctions, Account Revocation & Appeals

Violations of institutional policies, academic misconduct, or unauthorized system exploitation may lead to temporary account suspension, assessment nullification, disciplinary probation, or permanent revocation of ELMS portal access. Sanctioned users may file a written appeal through the ICC Academic Review Committee pursuant to student handbook due process.
